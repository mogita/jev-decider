Transaction Decider

A bank record goes in, a budget category comes out. Jev gave me the idea: the model never writes a sentence. It reads the record once, scores all 17 categories in that single pass, and returns the best one. So it cannot invent a category that is not on the list, and it takes the same time whether you offer two categories or twenty.

It runs on my laptop behind a tunnel, so please go easy on it. Nothing you type is stored.

Qwen3-4B + budget LoRA, merged, 7.5 GB bf16 · limits: 1 field / 10 records / 26 choices / 4000 chars · rate limit: 25 records per 10s, 100 per minute

Input

Leave a blank line between records to send up to 10 of them in one call.

One per line, 2 to 26. Leave them alone and the model answers against the trained budget categories. Change them and it answers against yours, measured at 86% on AG News and 47% on emotion, two public sets it never saw. The confidence is noticeably less honest off the trained categories, so treat it as a direction rather than a number.

Answer

Pick an example, or write your own.

Answered against your own categories, not the trained ones.
